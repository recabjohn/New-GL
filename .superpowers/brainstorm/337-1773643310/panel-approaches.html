B

Default Open (First Visit)

Panel is open by default for new users. Once they close it, their preference is saved (localStorage) and it stays closed on future visits.

First-time user sees →
SUBMISSIONS TABLE
NEEDS ATTENTION
DUE THIS WEEK
RECENT ACTIVITY
MY TASKS
💡 Panel is open by default. Click ⊠ to collapse and save your preference.
Returning user →
SUBMISSIONS TABLE (full width)
Panel hidden (user preference saved)
✓ Pros
  • Zero extra clicks — panel immediately visible
  • Simplest to build (just change default state)
  • Respects user preference after first visit
✗ Cons
  • Panel doesn't re-open on new sessions (localStorage persists close)
  • Still relies on icon button for returning users
C

Labeled Toggle Button with Live Counts

Replace the icon-only button with a small labeled button that shows live counts even when the panel is closed. Always communicates the value before clicking.

Button when panel is closed →

Export
+ New Submission
User sees "Alerts 4" and knows exactly what clicking will show
Button when panel is open →
Alerts 4 ◀
Export
+ New Submission
Button stays visible, active state shows panel is open
✓ Pros
  • Always communicates value (4 alerts visible even when closed)
  • Works permanently, not just first visit
  • One click to open — no extra steps
✗ Cons
  • "Alerts" label only hints at one of the 4 sections
  • Doesn't surface My Tasks, Due This Week, Activity

My recommendation: A (your idea) is the most informative on first open, and combining it with C's live alert count on the toggle button would make it even stronger. But the right call depends on how often your users will toggle the panel.
